HYG star data
=============
Data: HYG v4.0, David Nash / Astronexus.
Source: https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database/tree/main/hyg
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International.
See HYG-LICENSE.txt and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

stars.json is an adapted subset generated 2026-10-30. Rows contain:
[id, x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, V magnitude, B-V]
Positions: J2000 equatorial parsecs; velocities: parsecs/year.
Missing B-V is represented by 0.65 (solar-like assumed colour).
Selection: valid distance <100000 pc and (V <=7 or distance <=100 pc).
34,984 rows; Sun omitted here and added separately for exoplanet observers.
The derived catalogue is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

are documented in docs/science-model.md and docs/science-data-provenance.json.
